# ![Companions](img/logo.png)

Extra features for your furry and feathered friends. No client mod required!

## Features

### Wolf sweaters!
![wolf sweater recipe](img/wolf_sweater_recipe.gif)
![dyed wolf sweater recipe](img/dyed_wolf_sweater_recipe.gif)

Keep your friend cozy and warm in cold weather, or just make it a fashion statement!
Sweaters come in all 16 colors and can be combined with dye for a cute colored pattern on top of the wool color.
They don't protect as well as armadillo scute armor, but they protect from freezing and don't break.

### More features soon!
- a whistle to summon pets
- protect tamed mobs from friendly-fire
- more wolf sweaters

## Install

Download the .jar from [releases](releases/latest) and drop into your mods folder.
Only works on Fabric, and requires [Polymer](https://modrinth.com/mod/polymer).
The mod will not work on vanilla clients without Polymer's resource pack hosting enabled.
